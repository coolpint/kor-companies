Hyundai Motor India targets 20% women representation in executive workforce by 2030 About 45 per cent of young talent recruited by the automaker in 2026 are women, signalling changing workforce trends By PTI Updated - September 07, 2026 at 01:58 PM com/companies/hyundai-motor-india-targets-20-women-representation-in-executive-workforce-by-2030/article71437899 ece HMIL MD & CEO Tarun Garg | Photo Credit: cueapi Hyundai Motor India Ltd on Monday said it is targeting increasing women's representation in its executive workforce to 20 per cent by 2030 as part of its efforts to build a diverse and future-ready talent pipeline Women currently constitute more than 8 per cent of Hyundai Motor India Ltd's (HMIL) total executive workforce of over 4,000 employees, the company said in a statement At the same time, about 45 per cent of young talent (Management Trainees & Engineer Trainees) recruited in 2026 are women, underscoring changing talent trends, it added. 이 기사는 해당 기업과 연결된 해외 보도를 바탕으로 정리했다.
